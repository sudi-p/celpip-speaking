What is Writing Task 1?

You are asked to write an email in response to a situation described in the prompt. The email may be to a friend, neighbour, coworker, manager, or business — so tone can vary from informal to formal. You have 27 minutes total and are expected to write 150–200 words.

What does the prompt look like?

The prompt always includes a scenario and 3 bullet points telling you exactly what to include in your email. You must address ALL three bullet points clearly. Missing even one can significantly lower your score.

Common email scenarios

Complaint to a landlord or business · Apology to a friend or colleague · Request for information or help · Invitation or event planning · Giving advice · Responding to a job offer · Thank-you letter · Neighbourhood concern

Formal vs informal: how to decide

Read the recipient carefully. Writing to a friend or family member? Use informal, conversational language. Writing to a manager, company, or authority? Use polite, professional language. The prompt often gives you a clear signal — follow it.

What M9+ looks like

At M9 and above, all 3 bullet points are addressed, the tone perfectly matches the recipient, vocabulary is varied and precise, sentences are well-structured with natural connectors, and the email reads like something a fluent speaker would actually send. There are very few grammar slips and no awkward phrasing.
